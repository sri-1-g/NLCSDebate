How to Become a Member

Ready to boost your confidence, sharpen your speaking skills, and join thought-provoking discussions? Fill out the form to join our debate club—beginners and experienced debaters are all welcome!

Once you sign up, we’ll contact you with details about meetings, training sessions, and opportunities to participate in debates and competitions.

As a member, you'll get:

  • Access to exclusive resources
  • Regular practice sessions
  • Mentorship and personalized feedback
  • A chance to represent the club in school and inter-school events
Your journey to becoming a confident, persuasive speaker starts here. We can't wait to hear your voice!
