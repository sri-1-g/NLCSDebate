Our Missions and Values

About Our Debate Club

The Debate Society is a space for students who enjoy thinking deeply, speaking clearly, and challenging ideas—respectfully, of course. It's not just about winning arguments; it's about learning how to build them, break them down, and see the world from more than one angle.

Our main focus? Sharpening critical thinking, improving argumentation, and building confidence in persuasive speaking. Whether you're here to prep for competitions or just want to speak up more in class, there's a place for you.

Critical Thinking

We encourage logical reasoning, structured argumentation, and the ability to evaluate multiple perspectives on complex issues.

Confidence

Through regular speaking opportunities, members develop the courage to articulate their views clearly and assertively.

Respectful Discourse

We value civil discussions, where opposing viewpoints are welcomed and debated with mutual respect.

Research & Rigor

We promote rigorous research practices that support strong, evidence-backed argumentation on a wide range of topics.

Creativity & Clarity

Debating isn’t just about facts—it’s about delivering them with creativity and clarity that leaves a lasting impact.
